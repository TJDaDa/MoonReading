MoonReading Privacy Policy

Last Revised on October 8th, 2024

This Privacy Policy for MoonReading LLC (“Company”, “we”, “us”, “our”) describes how we collect, use, and disclose information about users of our website, applications, services, tools, and features (collectively, the “Services”). The Services are designed for users in the United States only.

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. By using the Services, you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use or access the Services.

1. Updating This Privacy Policy

We may modify this Privacy Policy and will update the “Last Revised” date. If we make material changes to how we use information, we will use reasonable efforts to notify you. It is your responsibility to review this policy from time to time.

2. Our Collection and Use of Information

When you use the Services, we may collect information you submit directly, including:

  • Basic contact details (name, phone, email) to provide your personalized moon reading, account access, and to communicate with you, including about products that may interest you. We may send emails from MoonReading.com, YourMoonGuide.com and MoonCards.com.
  • Payment information (collected by our payment processor) to process your payment and provide requested products or services.
  • Information relating to your visit, including data from cookies, pixels, and similar technologies, and from webforms and Service features.
  • Service-specific information such as date of birth, time of birth, location of birth, gender, and relationship status for your personalized moon reading.

We and third parties may also collect Usage Data (device information, IP address, browser type, online activity, etc.) through tracking technologies for analytics, tailored content, and advertising. We use this information to provide the Services, monitor trends, comply with legal obligations, and protect our rights and users.

3. How We Share Your Information

We share your information with service providers and vendors (cloud storage, security, CRM, marketing, analytics, payment processors, etc.). We share limited information with third party advertising partners and tracking technology providers for online advertising. We may also share information to comply with law, cooperate with law enforcement, in connection with a business transaction, within our corporate group, with professional advisors, or when you request us to share.

4. Cookies and Other Tracking Technologies

Most browsers accept cookies automatically. You may be able to control how your device permits tracking technologies. Blocking or deleting cookies may cause some Services to work incorrectly. To opt out of Google Analytics tracking, visit Google’s opt-out page. Our website may not respond to “Do Not Track” signals.

5. Third Party Websites and Links

We may provide links to third party websites or platforms. We are not responsible for the privacy, security, or content of those sites. Review their policies when you visit. Information you provide on third-party platforms may be viewable by others.

6. Children’s Privacy

Children are not permitted to use the Services. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we learn we have collected information from a child under 13, we will make reasonable efforts to delete it. Parents or guardians of a child under 13 who has provided personal information may contact us to request deletion.

7. Data Security

We use reasonable efforts to protect your information, but no security measures are impenetrable. Information sent electronically may not be secure in transit. We recommend you do not use unsecure channels for sensitive or confidential information.

8. Privacy Rights

Depending on your state and applicable law, you may have rights such as: Right to Access; Right to Delete; Right to Correct; Right to Opt-out of Sharing for Targeted Advertising (visit the Your Privacy Choices page); Right to Unsubscribe (use the unsubscribe link in our emails; you must unsubscribe from MoonReading.com, YourMoonGuide.com and MoonCards.com separately).

To exercise these rights, email us at support@moonreading.com. We may need to verify your identity. You may designate an authorized agent in writing. You may have the right to appeal our decision regarding a request.

9. How to Contact Us

For questions about our privacy practices or this Privacy Policy, please email us at support@moonreading.com.
